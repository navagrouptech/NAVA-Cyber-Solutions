Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Draft Document: This privacy policy reflects NAVA Cyber Solutions' privacy-by-design standards. Review and tailor to match specific production third-party processors prior to commercial deployment.

1. Our Commitment to Privacy

NAVA Cyber Solutions ("we", "our", or "the Company"), founded by ABHIRAM ALLADA, prioritizes the fundamental right to digital privacy. We operate on principles of data minimization: we do not collect information that is not strictly necessary to provide our cybersecurity services and web applications.

2. Information Collection & Usage

When you browse our website, we do not employ invasive tracking cookies or sale-of-data advertising networks. Information is collected only in the following contexts:

  • Contact Inquiries: When you voluntarily submit an inquiry through our contact form, we collect your name, email address, topic of interest, and message content solely to respond to your inquiry.
  • Interactive Tools (Guess Proof AI): Our heuristic password analyzer runs entirely client-side within your browser. Passwords evaluated in Guess Proof AI are never transmitted across the internet, logged to server databases, or saved in cookies.

3. Third-Party Hosting & Infrastructure

This website is planned for deployment on Firebase Hosting (Google Cloud). Firebase may record technical request headers (such as IP addresses and user-agent strings) for security inspection, DDOS mitigation, and content delivery network caching in accordance with Google's Cloud Privacy Notice.

4. Data Retention and Security

We implement industry-standard administrative and technical safeguards to protect any submitted communication records from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. We do not sell or rent user contact details to any third party.

5. Your Data Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information you have submitted to us. To exercise these rights, contact us at contact@navacybersolutions.com.
